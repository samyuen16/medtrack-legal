MedTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

MedTrack is a personal medication and supplement reminder and logging app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

What we collect

  • Medication data you enter: medicine names, dosages, schedules, notes, stock counts and dose logs (this can be health-related information).
  • Account data (optional): a username (or email address) and a password, only if you create a Cloud Sync account. Usernames have no password reset, so keep your password safe.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts or analytics, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where your data is stored

  • Local mode (default): everything stays on your device. No account is needed and no data is sent to us.
  • Cloud Sync (optional): your medications and dose logs are stored in a database hosted by Supabase so they can sync between your devices. Each record is protected by row-level security so only your account can read it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

How we use your data

Only to run the app: showing your schedule, sending the reminders you set, and syncing between your devices. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising or data mining.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete a medication, or all local data, in Settings → Your Data.
  • Delete your account and all cloud data in Settings → Your Data → Delete Account & Cloud Data. This is permanent.
  • You can also ask for deletion by emailing irdsamyuenai@gmail.com.

Retention

Cloud data is kept until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account removes it.

Children

MedTrack is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
