MedTrack Support

MedTrack is a personal medication and supplement reminder and logger. It is a reminder tool only and not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Contact

Questions, bugs or data requests: irdsamyuenai@gmail.com

Common questions

I am not getting reminders. Allow notifications for MedTrack in your device settings, and make sure the medicine is not paused and has reminders turned on.

How do I sync between devices? Settings → Storage → Cloud Sync, then sign in with the same account on each device.

How do I delete my data or account? Settings → Your Data.
